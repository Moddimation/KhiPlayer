Discord Status (Mobile)

Show what you're listening to on your Discord profile.

Signing in grants KHInsider the sdk.social_layer_presence permission. This app only uses it to post your current track as your Discord activity -- it does not read messages, DMs, or your friends list. That said, the permission itself is broader than that (it's the only self-serve way Discord currently offers for this), so it's worth knowing what you're approving.

Read before continuing.

  • This build uses your Discord account token directly, not a normal login. It was built this way deliberately (--token build flag) rather than through Discord's sanctioned login.
  • Automating your account this way goes against Discord's Terms of Service.
  • The token is as sensitive as your password. It stays in this device's secure storage and is used only to open a status connection to Discord.
